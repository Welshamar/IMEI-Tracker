How location works here: this page never queries a phone number, IMEI or email to find a device. Each entry below gets a unique check-in link — send it to whoever holds that device. When they open it and tap "share location," their browser reports its own GPS coordinates, which show up here as a "suspected location" circle sized to the GPS accuracy reported by that device.
